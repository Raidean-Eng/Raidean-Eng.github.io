Video Agent – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Video Agent is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It creates short videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data it uses

The app asks for one Google permission: youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the signed-in channel. It does not read, change, or delete any other data in your Google account.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token is saved only in a file (token.json) on the owner's computer. No data is sent to the developer or to any other server, except Google/YouTube when a video is uploaded.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete token.json from the app folder.

Terms of use

This app is provided as-is for the owner's personal use only.
